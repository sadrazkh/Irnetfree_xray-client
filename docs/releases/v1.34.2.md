# v1.34.2 — a group never runs on a core whose loopback cuts the end of a download

By the version rule: a fix to something that already exists (groups on an old official core) — the third number.

## Why

v1.34.1's chain relay keeps a chain's hops from dropping the end of a closed connection. A group is a loopback
(configBuilder.expandGroups) into its balancer, and on an official Xray core before 26.4.25 the loopback outbound
copied through a pipe of its own (`dispatcher.Dispatch`) until XTLS/Xray-core#6005 (454c930) handed the link on
(`DispatchLink`). That pipe drops the same tail when the members ride WebSocket, and the relay cannot reach it: on
26.3.27, 1 of 100 256 KB pages through a plain group and 40 of 40 through `[hop → group]`; on Windows with
GOMAXPROCS=1, 0 of 40 whole through `[hop → group]` and `[group → hop]` alike, where Xray-PattN 26.10.19 carried
all 40 (scripts/probe-chain-tail.js). The owner's desktop runs official 26.3.27 and Xray-PattN 26.10.19.

## The gate (the owner's choice: move it, else warn)

`xrayManager.validateWithFallback` (`GROUP_LOOPBACK_SINCE`, `runsGroup`):

- A config with a group on an Xray core older than 26.4.25 moves to the other Xray core when that one says
  26.4.25 or newer and takes the config — said in the log.
- Otherwise it runs where it was asked, never refused (a group still carries nearly everything, and a router on its
  opkg core, 24.12.31 / 25.1.30, would have none), and the connect log says, in the user's language, which version
  to update to.
- Nothing moves to the official core that it would refuse or drop (the fork's finalmask), or when the other core's
  check fails.

## Proven

npm test (2763: tests/groupCore.test.js, xrayManager and serviceGateway cases); CI on both cores.
