# v1.28.0 — Chains & groups in tabs, picks by subscription, a group's members in the flow

## What the owner asked for

"Groups beside chains is good, but scrolling breaks it"; open/close for the chain and group sections, or tabs; the
configs in the pick lists split by subscription like the Servers page; in the routing diagram, how much goes through
each member of a group — or a small box on hover / click showing each one's share, and clicking goes there. "Show me
the design first, then build — nothing else may break." The sample (`.superpowers/mockups/chains-groups.html`) was
approved as is: the flow's box opens on hover and stays on click; the card of the group in use opens by itself.

The scroll report's cause: every chain card listed every server under it — fifteen servers made a 4,000 px page.

## What it does

- **«زنجیره و گروه» in two tabs** — «⛓ زنجیره‌ها» and «⧉ گروه‌ها» with their counts; the head button follows the tab;
  «همه را باز کن / همه را ببند».
- **Cards fold to one line** — name, a summary (a chain's hops, a group's size and strategy), pings, actions; a click
  opens it. The tab and every card's state are remembered; the chain or group you are connected to opens by itself.
- **Picks by subscription** — the chain's «افزودن حلقه» and the group editor's «+ کانفیگ» list configs folded by
  subscription like the Servers page (manual first, groups last), in a box with its own scroll; a search unfolds
  what matches; a config already in the chain is dimmed.
- **A group's members, live** — on the group's card: its speed and a share bar; opened, one row per member with the
  core's own health check (alive / dead, its delay), its speed and its share of the traffic; a dead member red, a
  removed one grey; a row takes you to that config.
- **In the flow** (the home page's «مسیر ترافیک» and the Routing page's tree) — a node that carries a running group
  gets a thin share bar and «alive/total» without growing; hovering opens a small box with each member's share,
  a click pins it, Esc or a click outside closes it; a member row goes to that config, the foot to the group's card.

## Underneath

- `configBuilder.expandGroups` records who each `T~gm<i>` is (the server dialled; a chain variant by the group member
  it takes); `buildConfig` / `buildPoolConfig` keep it beside the config as a non-enumerable `groupTags` — never
  written to disk.
- main.js and the router hand it to the StatsPoller at every connect; the poller reads each member's counters and
  `vars.observatory` (Xray's health check: alive, delay) and sends `groups` with every tick while a group runs.
- `scripts/probe-group.js` checks it on the real cores in all five rounds: the dead member dead, the others alive.
- Renderer: the page, the folds, the picker, the live rows, the flow marks and the floating box (position: fixed,
  never clipped by its panel), each with its tests (tests/groupsUi2.test.js).

## Not proven here

Real servers and real traffic; the Android screens are unchanged this round.
