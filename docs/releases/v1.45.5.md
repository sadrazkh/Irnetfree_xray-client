# v1.45.5 — The cut hello everywhere it was missing

By the version rule: fixes and improvements to what already exists, so the third number goes up.

## What was left out in v1.45.3

v1.45.3 cut the TLS hello of a config's first hop when the path reads names (`src/main/helloCut.js`). Three places did not have it.

## What changed

- **A chain's later hops.** A hop behind another comes out at the previous hop's server, which reads its name just as a router's exit does: a Reality or pinned second hop died. With the cut on, such a hop dials through a freedom that cuts its hello and itself dials the hop in front, over the chain relay when there is one (`relayHopDials`), so the fix for the tail lost through a `ws` hop still holds. A hop behind a WireGuard, a freedom or a loopback is not cut.
- **Configs on the sing-box engine.** `tls.record_fragment` (sing-box 1.12 and later; an older or unknown version gets nothing). `tls.fragment` is not enough: measured, once a relay puts the segments back into one write, the reader reads the name again.
- **Android.** The same check at a connect with no VPN up (two TLS dials, 2 s, off the main thread), the same cut, and no certificate learned while the path reads names.
- **The ▶ wording.** v1.45.3 said the tunnel stays up on a switch. It does not: it is rebuilt for the new plan, and an armed kill switch seals the gap. The button's hint, the README and the v1.45.3 notes say so now.

## Tested

- `scripts/probe-hello-cut.js`, both suggested cores and sing-box 1.14.4, real cores on loopback: a chain's second hop, pinned and Reality, dies behind a first hop that reads and passes cut, with and without the relay; sing-box TLS dies whole and passes with `record_fragment`; the real site is not dialled.
- `tests/helloCut.test.js`, `tests/singboxBuilder.test.js`, `tests/tunSingbox.test.js`; `HelloCutTest.kt` for Android.

## Limits and owed

- **Android is compiled only in CI** and has not run on a phone.
- **Reality on the sing-box engine is unproved:** sing-box 1.14.4's Reality client did not authenticate against an Xray Reality server even with nothing in between.
- **A LAN user switched to a stored WireGuard while connected** (router) runs before that WireGuard's address is in the tunnel's bypass; a reconnect puts it there.
- Nothing here ran on his real servers in a chain, or on a phone.
