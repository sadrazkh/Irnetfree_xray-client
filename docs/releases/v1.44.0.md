# v1.44.0 — Traffic by site on the router, device by device

By the version rule: a new capability, so the second number goes up.

## What the owner asked

v1.43.0's traffic by site was desktop only, and he expected it on the router too: «آره بساز، به تفکیک هر دستگاه هم
باشه، قابل روشن خاموش کردن باشه که اگر به دستگاه فشار اومد خاموشش کنم، خیلی فشار نیار به دستگاه و حافظه و سی‌پی‌یو و
رم». So: by device, with a switch, and light on the router.

## What is new

- **The router's Logs page has the tab** (the Connections tab stays desktop only), with a device picker: the whole
  house, or one device, listed by name with its total.
- **By device:** a connection's source address names its device, by MAC where the router knows it, so a phone keeps its
  rows across a new DHCP lease. An address not yet known stands in, and its rows move to the device once the list knows
  it. The router's own traffic is a device of its own.
- **The switch:** off, nothing is read at once, and the next connect has no API and no sniff, so sing-box is back to
  exactly what it was. Off is the default.

## How it stays light

- **sing-box:** the API exists only while the option is on (a desktop always has it), and without `find_process`: a
  router's connections belong to devices, not programs, so looking one up for each would be for nothing.
- **CPU:** a read every 5 s rather than every second, further apart when a read takes over 2 % of the time, up to a
  minute. A read's cost leaves the device list out. The device list is read every 2 minutes; a new address earns one
  early look, so an address the list never has does not keep the router spawning `ip`. Old days are pruned once a day.
- **Flash:** a file of its own (site-usage.json), written every 30 minutes and at a disconnect or stop. A power cut
  loses at most the last half hour of figures.
- **Memory:** bounded by 31 days of the house at 200 rows a day, and 14 days of 64 devices at 30 rows a day. Past a
  ceiling, a new site joins Other, and bytes are never lost from the totals. Unresolved addresses may take at most half
  the day's device places.
- **Its cost is on the page:** "last read: N connections in M ms, once every S s", for deciding whether to turn it off.

## Limits

- A device excluded from the VPN does not enter the TUN, so it is not here.
- Reads 5 s apart mean more short connections land in Other than on a desktop. Other has no device.
- Anyone who can open the router's page sees which sites each device used.
- The Logs page is pro-only, as before.

## Tested

- `npm test`: tests/siteUsage.test.js (each device's rows, ceilings, the save interval, the back-off, the router's
  config with and without the option).
- **scripts/probe-router-site-usage.js** in CI, the real TUN on both sing-box lines: a router namespace running the
  router's own config, two devices in namespaces of their own. Each one's page lands in its own rows and not the
  other's, by the app's own meter, and the picker lists them by name.
- **The QEMU smoke**, the real package on OpenWrt: off, the router's sing-box has no API; on and reconnected, it has the
  API and the sniff and no process lookups, it records, a device in a LAN namespace has its own rows under its MAC, and
  off stops it at once. A read took 32 ms there, with 6 connections on an emulated CPU.

## Owed

The owner's AC-1304 with the house on it: whether the load stays unnoticeable. The page shows each read's cost.
