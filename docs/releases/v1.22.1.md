# v1.22.1 — The home flow's lines stay on their boxes

## What the owner reported (v1.22.0)

- As the numbers changed, the picture shifted left and right.
- Lines ran through the middle of boxes: one went in mid-box, another came out a little past its box. This got worse as
  the window was resized.
- The colours should not run out for someone with more routes.

## Why

- The device's column was as wide as its live speed. Each second's new figure moved every other column, but the lines
  were redrawn only when the panel itself changed size. So they stayed where they had been drawn.
- A rule going straight to its target was drawn as one curve across the base column, under the base's box.

## The fix

- **The device's column has a width of its own.** The live speed is written down over up, on two lines, so the column
  never follows the numbers. A target's figures have room for the widest one, so its label does not twitch.
- **The lines follow every column.** They are redrawn whenever any column changes size: a number, a font arriving, the
  window. A headless Chrome check measured every line's ends at six window widths with the numbers changing:
  0 px off.
- **A line never cuts through a base.** A line past the bases column goes around a box in its way, level through the
  nearest clear height, in before the column and out after it. The routing page's tree does the same.
- **Colours without end.** Five chosen hues (sky, violet, amber, pink, coral), then as many more as there are targets,
  never near direct's green or block's red. The theme sets their lightness.
- **The arrows follow the reading direction:** → in English, ← in Persian (the stacked layout, the tooltips).

## Unchanged

Every config, byte for byte: renderer only.
