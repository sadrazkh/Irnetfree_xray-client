# v1.30.0 — Android in Persian

By the version rule: a new capability — the Android app speaks the owner's language — the second number.

## What the owner asked for

«ادامه بده فارسی اندروید و گروه اندروید رو تموم کن». The group part shipped as v1.29.1; this is the Persian part.

## What it does (Android)

- **Every screen in Persian** — Home, Servers, subscriptions, Chains & groups, Routing (profiles, rules, the flow),
  Settings, LAN sharing, the WARP and WireGuard sheets, the server editor (with the desktop's own Persian labels),
  the notification and the Quick Settings tile: 644 words of the app's own, 206 of the editor's. The terms are the
  desktop's (زنجیره، گروه، ساب، پایه، روتینگ ویژه، گلوبال، دور زدن ایران …), so both apps say the same thing.
- **Right to left** — the layout mirrors in Persian; back arrows and chevrons turn; a route reads «بقیه ← …».
  Addresses, ports, keys, links, JSON, the logs and the brand stay left to right.
- **A language switch** — فارسی / English at the top of Settings, applied at once; Persian by default.
- **Not translated, on purpose** — the log lines (More → Logs is what the owner sends the developer), messages that
  come from the cores, and technical labels (MTU, Reserved, pbk/sid, protocol names).

## Underneath

- core/I18n.kt (t/tf, the language in Compose-observable state, values set in their own direction inside a Persian
  sentence), core/I18nWords.kt; scripts/gen-android-server-fields.js writes the editor's STRINGS_FA from the desktop's
  fa table.
- I18nTest: every key in both languages, none empty, the same placeholders, tf fills, the RTL arrow.

## Not proven here

On the phone: every screen in Persian, the switch, the right-to-left layout on a real device.
