# v1.34.0 — users on a hotspot that just works, caps by the Persian month, a router no device can fool

By the version rule: new capabilities (a phone's link and QR per user, caps by the Persian month or a renew day,
addresses pinned to their devices) — the second number.

## Why the hotspot did not work

Read on the owner's laptop (read-only): a user `sadra` with a 2 GB monthly cap, and proxy accounts OFF — nothing
listened on 30820/30821. The firewall was not it: Windows already lets IRNetFree's xray.exe in on Public networks,
where the hotspot is. Nothing on the page said the switch mattered.

- Users there and no way in for any of them: a line above everything says so, with the button that turns the
  proxy on (on the router, when the transparent mode is off too).
- The hotspot's address (192.168.137.1) first and marked; when it is missing the page says the hotspot is off.
- 🔗 beside each user: a `socks://` link (base64url of user:pass — the form the app's own parsers and
  v2rayN/v2rayNG read) and its QR. Imported on a phone, every app rides the account, where a Wi-Fi proxy reached the
  browser alone and Android's never asks for a password. The QR is qrcode.js's own image: no markup in the page.
- sing-box's own TUN adapter (172.19.0.1, named IRNetFree) was offered as an address to give people, and on a
  10.x LAN, LAN sharing's lanIp picked it. Our adapters are skipped by name now (isOwnTunInterface).

## The rail keeps every item in view

In the labelled skin at the window's opening size (1080×720) the last items — Extensions, Settings — ran past the
rail's end. They shrink together now (`--navz`, every item, gap and padding as one) and grow back with the window,
never past their own size; a labelled skin drops its labels first where they would be unreadable (below 0.75), and
past 0.55 the rail scrolls. The flex column no longer squeezes a button to its icon. tests/navFit.test.js renders
three skins × Simple/Pro × both directions × five sizes: every item inside the rail, none over another.

## Caps by the Persian month, or a renew day

`quotaCalendar` ('gregorian' | 'jalali') and `quotaResetDay` (1–31) on a user's cap and a link's, stored only when
not the default — an upgrade resets no one. Keys `jYYYY-MM`, `YYYY-MM@D`, `jYYYY-MM@D` beside the old ones; one
roll rule (a month keeps the bytes of a period it overlaps: changing the calendar never hands out a fresh cap).
src/main/jalali.js is plain arithmetic — the router's Node may have small ICU — checked day by day against Intl's
Persian calendar 1990–2080. Notices say the real renew date.

## The router: an address is its device's

sing-box picks a device's config by its source address; the gate let a device through by its own MAC. So one let
through could take another's address and ride that user's config and cap. Every address of a user's device is now
pinned to its MAC in the gateway's table (`lu_bound4/6`, `lu_ipmac4/6`, concatenated sets) — a packet from it with
any other MAC is dropped, before the gate and the counters. One owner per address (a running lease's address is its
holder's). The sign-in page asks who is asking now (a fresh scan, the neighbour table before the lease).

## Smaller

- Multi-internet: a share written as the fewest copies (2:2 is 1:1), probes every 30 s, 60 s on a capped link:
  ~100 MB a day per link → ~17 (estimated). A dropped link is skipped ~35 s later instead of ~15 s.
- The groups box shows a weighted link once (×N), alive if any copy is.
- The version picker lists GitHub's releases 10 at a time, a next page only when needed (PattN a third of the
  bytes, sing-box two thirds; Xray still three pages).
