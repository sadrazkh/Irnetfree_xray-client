## The CI flakes: one cause

The probes listened on fixed ports 39700–41900 — inside Linux's ephemeral range (32768–60999), the ports the kernel
hands every outgoing connection. Two things followed, now and then, on either core:

- **"bind: address already in use"** — a fixed port was already the local end of a live loopback connection.
  probe-warp-chain's 127.0.0.1:40112 was taken for both of its rounds, 8 s apart (and MASQUE's before it).
- **probe-group's "no HTTP answer"** — its dead member points at a port nobody listens on; inside the ephemeral range
  a connect there can reach itself (TCP self-connect), so the dead member answered the health check now and then, the
  round-robin picked it, and one fetch in nine got nothing. Not PattN's and not the app's: the same on 26.10.21.

Every base is 18000 lower now (21600–23900, the same spacing). tests/probePorts.test.js keeps every probe's ports
below 32768, clear of the app's own, and no two runs in the workflow overlapping.

## The router's page on a phone

- `<meta name="viewport">`: the router's web page (Users, Extensions) opens at the phone's width instead of a
  zoomed-out desktop.
- Settings no longer scrolls sideways at 390 px: the four TUN selects hidden behind their option cards took the full
  width under a narrow window's `.select { width: 100% }` (537 px of 390). Every page now measures 390 at 390.

## Proven

CI green on every job with the new ports; npm test (probePorts).
