# v1.46.0 — Rows that move with the mouse

By the version rule: a new capability, so the second number goes up.

## What the owner asked

To move the routing rows with the mouse, mostly for looks, on the Routing page and in Home's traffic path, the configs in the middle too, and even the fixed rows such as Iran. Then: a choice of whether a move only changes the look or also the real order.

## What changed (desktop and the router's page)

- **Every row of the flow moves**, on the Routing page and on Home: the user's rules, the fixed rows (ads, local network, Iran, everything else) and the bases. A grip on each row, a drop line where it will land, and Alt+Up / Alt+Down on a focused row.
- **A switch by the flow's title:** *Display only* (the default) or *Real order too*, kept per viewer.
  - Display only: only the picture changes, kept per viewer. Routing is untouched.
  - Real order too: moving your own rules changes their real order. On the Routing page it is an edit like any other and Save applies it; on Home it is saved at once, and refused while that routing has unsaved edits on the Routing page.
  - The fixed rows and the bases move on the picture only, in both modes: their place in the core does not change. Their hints say so.
- **When the picture differs from the real order**, a line under the flow says so, with a button that puts the real order back on screen.
- **The rule list** has a grip on every row: dragging it changes the real order, since that list is the editor.

## Tested

- `tests/routingUi.test.js`: display and real modes, a merged group, the fixed rows, the note and its reset, the keyboard, bases kept and forgotten when stale, the editor with and without profiles, Home's save and its refusal.
- The real page in headless Chrome, Persian and English: dispatched drags moved a rule to first and last in the tree, the editor and Home.

## Limits

- Not tried with a real mouse in the desktop app, or on a phone. Android has no flow tree.
- The picture's order is kept in the browser or window that made it, not shared between devices.
