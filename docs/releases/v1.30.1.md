# v1.30.1 — the local ports move to the 30000s

By the version rule: a default that changes, nothing new to do — the third number.

## What the owner asked for

«پورت پیشفرض هم کلا بجای 10808 و اینا ببر رو رنج 3 یعنی مثلا 30808 که اختلال نخوره با برنامه های مشابه» —
10808/10809/10085 are what v2rayN, v2rayNG, Nekoray and most Xray clients take, so two of them on one machine fought
over the same ports.

## What it does

- **New defaults** — SOCKS `30808`, HTTP `30809`, stats `30085` on the desktop, the router and Android; Android's LAN
  sharing `30810`/`30811` for a new install.
- **An existing install moves once, by itself** (settingsMigrate.js `migratePorts`; Android `AppSettings.movedPort`):
  only a port still on its OLD DEFAULT moves, a port the user chose stays, and the `portsMoved` mark keeps a later
  10808 a choice the next start leaves alone.
- **What another device points at is not moved** — with LAN sharing on (`allowLan`) the desktop's and the router's
  SOCKS/HTTP ports stay where they were (the stats port is loopback-only and moves); Android's LAN sharing ports are
  stored apart and kept.
- **The system proxy** follows on the next connect; a dead session's leftover on the old port is still repaired at
  launch (the journal records the port it wrote).

## Underneath

- The defaults in configBuilder.js, main.js, service.js, singboxBuilder.js, stats.js, sysproxy.js, the renderer's
  fallbacks, the remote agent, Android's Models.kt and XrayVpnService.
- tests/settingsMigrate.test.js: the move, a chosen port, the mark, LAN sharing on, the defaults of desktop and router
  alike. PortsTest.kt: the same on the phone. desktopPin's v1.16.1 pin gives v1.16.1's ports.

## Not proven here

On a real machine: the move on the first start after the update (Settings shows 30808/30809), the system proxy on
127.0.0.1:30809 after a connect; on the phone, the same in Settings.
